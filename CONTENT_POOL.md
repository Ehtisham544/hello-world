# SIC content pool — read before choosing ANY topic (Shorts, long videos, community posts)

The channel covers ALL funded opportunities in China, not only CSC. Rotate across these categories and never cover the same category twice in a row. Over any 7 days of Shorts, at least 5 different categories must appear, and CSC content must be under 40% of posts.

## Categories
1. **CSC / Chinese Government Scholarship**: Type A (bilateral), Type B (university program), plus special CSC tracks (Silk Road, High-Level Graduate, EU Window, AUN, PIF, Great Wall/UNESCO, WMO, etc. as they exist and are verified).
2. **Provincial & city government scholarships**: e.g. Beijing, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Guangdong, Sichuan, Hubei, Shaanxi, Chongqing, Yunnan, Tianjin government scholarships.
3. **University scholarships**: president's scholarships, university excellence awards, freshman scholarships, partial-to-full tuition waivers at specific universities.
4. **Confucius Institute / International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship**: language year, semester, MTCSOL.
5. **MOFCOM / development-focused programs**: government-funded master's & PhD for officials and professionals from developing countries (as verified each year).
6. **Elite & special programs**: Schwarzman Scholars (Tsinghua), Yenching Academy (Peking University), Belt and Road / Silk Road programs, ASEAN, Africa-focused programs, joint programs with foreign universities.
7. **Interesting degree programs**: unique English-taught programs (AI, robotics, Chinese medicine, MBBS, international relations, tourism, film, aerospace, high-speed rail, agriculture).
8. **Fellowships & research visits**: visiting scholar programs, research fellowships, summer schools, short-term exchange, CAS-TWAS President's Fellowship, Chinese Academy of Sciences programs, ANSO scholarships.
9. **Postdoctoral positions**: university/CAS postdoc programs, international postdoc exchange programs, typical funding (verify per institution).
10. **Careers after studying in China**: jobs after a PhD (faculty positions, talent programs as verified), work permits for graduates, internships, teaching, working in China for international graduates.
11. **How-to / tips** (mostly Slot C Shorts): professor emails, documents, study plan, interviews, visa (X1/X2), arrival, life on a stipend, choosing cities/universities, common mistakes.
12. **Student life & cost**: cities, dorms, food, part-time rules, health insurance, costs vs stipend.

## Rules
- Every fact (amounts, deadlines, eligibility, agency numbers, ages) must be verified on an official source fetched in that run. If a program's current-year details can't be confirmed, say "check the official page for this year's deadline" or pick another topic.
- Prefer opportunities with open or upcoming deadlines; mention the deadline when known.
- Keep the audience global: students and researchers from every country.
- Log the category with each post (e.g. `[cat 8]`) so rotation can be checked.
